ASSESSMENT AREA 05

Agile Logbook

A record of work completed, next steps, blockers and learning.

Evidence

Add your actual dated logbook entries or screenshots. Keep entries consistent with the project timeline.

Suggested entry format

Date: [Actual date]

Planned work: [What you intended to do]

Work completed: [What you actually did]

Blockers/support needed: [Any issues, or “None” if accurate]

Next steps: [What you planned next]

Reflection: [What you learned or would improve]

My reflection

Reflection prompts

  • How did the logbook help you monitor progress?
  • How did you respond when plans changed?
  • What patterns did you notice in your work?
  • How will you improve your planning and reporting?

Use genuine dates and events. If you are reconstructing older entries, label them honestly as retrospective notes instead of presenting them as contemporaneous records.
