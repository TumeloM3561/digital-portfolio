Product Backlog

A prioritised list of work needed for the product.

Evidence

Add a screenshot/export/link to your actual product backlog. Ensure the priorities and current status are readable.

What to include

  • Feature, requirement or user story
  • Priority or ordering
  • Current status
  • Notes about changes made as the project developed

My reflection

Reflection prompts

  • How were items prioritised?
  • What changed in the backlog and why?
  • How did the backlog help the team understand the scope?
  • What would you improve in future backlog refinement?

Use the version of the backlog you actually worked with and explain your own contribution.
