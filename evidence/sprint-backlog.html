ASSESSMENT AREA 04

Sprint Backlog

Show the work selected for a sprint and how progress was tracked.

Evidence

Add your actual sprint goal, selected backlog items, taskboard screenshot and sprint dates.

What to include

  • Sprint goal
  • Selected stories/tasks
  • Owner or responsibility where recorded
  • Progress or status evidence
  • Review of completed and unfinished work

My reflection

Reflection prompts

  • Was the sprint goal clear and achievable?
  • What work did you personally complete?
  • What blocked or delayed progress?
  • What would you plan differently next time?

Only report sprint dates, completion and blockers that match your actual project records.
