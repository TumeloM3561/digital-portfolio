Agile Team Charter

Evidence and personal reflection on the team's shared purpose and ways of working.

Evidence

Add your actual Agile Team Charter here. You can link a document or insert a screenshot of the charter.

What the evidence should show

  • Project purpose and shared goal
  • Team roles and responsibilities
  • Agreed communication and meeting practices

My reflection

Write in your own words

  • What was your role and contribution?
  • How did the charter help the team work together?
  • What challenge did you experience and how was it handled?
  • What would you improve next time?

Replace these prompts with a reflection based on your real experience. Add evidence that supports what you say.
